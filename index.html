Integrated Thresholds

• ACSM Running: VO₂ = (0.2 × S) + (0.9 × S × G) + 3.5

• VT1 / LT1: Aerobic inflection; V̇E/VO₂ rises while V̇E/VCO₂ stays flat (~RER 0.85, [La⁻] ≈ 2.0 mM).

• VT2 / RCP / LT2: Hyperventilation threshold; V̇E/VCO₂ surges, [La⁻] > 4.0 mM, RER > 1.00.
