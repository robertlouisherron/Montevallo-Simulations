Integrated Thresholds & Calorimetry

• ACSM Running: VO₂ = (0.2 × S) + (0.9 × S × G) + 3.5

• Lactate Threshold (LT₁): The first point where [La⁻] begins to rise above resting levels (~2.0 mmol/L, V̇E/VO₂ inflection, RER ≈ 0.85).

• Onset of Blood Lactate Accumulation (OBLA / LT₂): The intensity at which blood lactate reaches 4.0 mmol/L, marking severe metabolic acidosis and hyperventilation (V̇E/VCO₂ surge, RER > 1.00).
